NOpartisan

No left. No right. No thanks.

Political feed filtering happens on your device. No accounts, analytics, or uploaded posts.

Uncheck to hide matching posts and trends completely. Hidden content will have no Reveal button.

Matches whole words and phrases, regardless of case. Broad terms can hide unrelated posts. Enable placeholders to reveal individual filtered posts.

GitHub receives your IP address when you download a list. Feed content and browsing history are never sent. Leave this empty to use the bundled list offline.

Initial support: Reddit, X, and Facebook web feeds. Text only; images, video, and changing site layouts can limit coverage.
